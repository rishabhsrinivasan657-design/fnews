Zero-coupon convertible bonds are a unique financial instrument that has gained traction in recent years, particularly amid the surge of interest in artificial intelligence (AI). These bonds do not pay periodic interest, meaning investors do not receive regular payments. Instead, they are sold at a discount to their face value and can be converted into a predetermined number of shares of the issuing company at maturity.

This structure allows companies to raise capital without incurring high interest costs, making it an attractive option for businesses looking to fund growth and innovation. The conversion feature provides investors with the potential to benefit from the company's future success, as they can convert their bonds into shares if the company's stock price rises.
