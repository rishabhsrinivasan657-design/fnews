Net debt adjustments are a financial concept that plays a crucial role in corporate acquisitions, which occur when one company buys another. This adjustment involves calculating a company's total debt and subtracting its cash and cash equivalents (liquid assets that can be quickly converted to cash). The result gives a clearer picture of what the company actually owes, which is essential for determining its value during a sale.

Understanding net debt adjustments is important for everyday people because these calculations can significantly impact the price a buyer pays for a company. If a company has high debt, the buyer may negotiate a lower purchase price to account for those financial obligations. This can affect shareholders, employees, and the overall market.
